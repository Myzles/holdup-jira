Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Holdup: Time in Status for Jira ("the app") runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. We do not run servers of our own.

What the app reads

Issue keys, summaries, types, assignees, created and resolution dates, statuses and status history. The report and issue sidebar read these in your browser, as you, and do not save them anywhere.

What the app stores

Per-project alert settings (threshold in days, emails on or off), and for each issue it has emailed about, the time of that email so it doesn't repeat it within a week. This is kept in Forge storage inside Atlassian's platform and is removed when the app is uninstalled. Stored data stays on Atlassian's platform.

What we collect

Nothing. The app calls no outside service, uses no analytics or tracking, sets no cookies and sends no data to us. We cannot see your issues.

Emails

If a project admin turns on stuck-work emails, the app asks Jira to notify an issue's assignee. Jira sends the email and applies its own notification rules.

Atlassian

Atlassian hosts the app and handles billing and licensing. See the Atlassian Privacy Policy.
