Common questions

Why is cycle time empty? Cycle time needs finished issues that passed through an in-progress status. If your workflow goes straight from To Do to Done, only lead time is shown.

Why don't the numbers match another tool? Check two things: Holdup counts calendar time unless Skip weekends is ticked, and it measures cycle time from the first in-progress status to the last time the issue reached done.

A status shows as a number. That status was deleted from Jira. Its time is still counted under the name it had in the issue history where possible.

I can't save alert settings. Only project admins can change them.

The report says it stopped at 5,000 issues. Use a shorter period, pick an issue type, or add extra JQL.
